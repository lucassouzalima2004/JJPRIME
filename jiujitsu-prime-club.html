Meet the Founder

Professor Stefan Stoianov

HEAD COACH & FOUNDER — JIUJITSU PRIME CLUB
BLACK BELT — 4TH DEGREE

Stefan Stoianov began training Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu in 1998 in São Paulo, Brazil — one of the world's most competitive BJJ environments. After moving to California in 2009, he earned his black belt in 2011 and went on to train extensively with Kron Gracie, while also learning from Rickson Gracie. An active competitor since white belt, Stefan continues to train and evolve alongside top-level athletes. As the founder of Jiujitsu Prime Club in Venice, he delivers refined, high-level Jiu-Jitsu in a structured, beginner-friendly environment.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

Do I need experience to start?
Not at all. Our Fundamentals program is specifically designed for beginners with zero experience. You'll start with the most foundational concepts and build from there in a safe, supportive environment.
Is this Gracie Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu?
Yes. Prime Club is rooted in the Gracie Jiu-Jitsu methodology — the original and most complete self-defense-based system in the world. Our curriculum and lineage trace directly to the Gracie family.
Do you offer beginner classes?
Yes — the Adults Fundamentals class is dedicated entirely to beginners and white belts. Classes are structured so that every new student knows exactly what to expect and can follow along from day one.
Do you have kids classes?
Yes. Our Kids Program is available for ages 5–15 and runs multiple times per week. Classes focus on discipline, confidence, and physical coordination through age-appropriate Jiu-Jitsu instruction.
What should I wear for my first class?
For your first free trial, comfortable athletic wear is perfectly fine — shorts and a t-shirt work great. If you continue training, we'll help you get properly fitted for a gi. No need to purchase anything beforehand.
Do you offer sauna access?
Yes. Sauna access is included in our All Access and Elite membership tiers. It's available after training hours and is a popular part of the Prime Club experience for members focused on recovery.
Where are you located?
We're located at 2494 Lincoln Blvd, Venice, CA. Convenient to Venice Beach, Santa Monica, and the surrounding Westside neighborhoods. Street parking and nearby lots available.
How do memberships work?
Memberships are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You can pause, upgrade, or cancel at any time. We believe in earning your membership every month — not locking you in.
